IEEE Access · Volume 13 · pp. 60270–60283 (2025)

Toward Real-Time Recognition of Continuous Indian Sign Language: A Multi-Modal Approach Using RGB and Pose

M. Geetha, N. Aloysius, D. A. Somasundaran, Amritha Raghunath, and P. Nedungadi

Methodology & Architecture: Continuous sign language recognition has historically suffered from computational latency, signer variability, and rapid co-articulation. SignFlow addresses this through a dual-stream architecture:

  • ▸ Dual-Stream Pretraining: Isolated ISL videos pretrain a 3D CNN, while MediaPipe pose landmarks pretrain a Transformer encoder.
  • ▸ Dynamic Downsampling: Temporally reduces video inputs to 6 fps, eliminating redundant frames while retaining critical linguistic transitions.
  • ▸ Alignment & Loss: Connectionist Temporal Classification (CTC) loss eliminates the need for expensive frame-level gloss annotations.
